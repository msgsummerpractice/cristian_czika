Dog Gallery

This is a gallery of dogs

Golden Retriever

Description: : Originally bred as gundogs for retrieving waterfowl, Golden Retrievers are famous for their gentle "soft mouths" and eager-to-please attitudes.

Border Collie

Description: Widely considered the most intelligent of all domestic dogs, Border Collies possess an intense herding instinct. They use a famous "herding eye"—an intense stare—to control livestock.

French Bulldog

Description: Lovingly nicknamed the "Frenchie," the French Bulldog was bred in Paris in the mid-19th century to be a miniature companion dog. They are incredibly quiet, rarely bark, and thrive on human companionship.
